## 9.1 Skin 0 Identity, Engine-Free Level-0 Layering, and Early Public Teaser (`CL-09-01`, `D-02`, `D-81`)

Publicly named **DNA Calc - Formula Explorer** (retaining `OneCalc` as its internal crate/module identifier, `dc-sdzi1`), **Formula Explorer (`dna-view-explorer`, `skin-0`)** is a specialized **tool**—not a sixth workbook view—built strictly over **Host Level 0** `[FRAMING-0025, FRAMING-0026, FRAMING-0051]`:

1. **Mechanical Level-0 ceiling (`D-81`, `SKIN_ARCHITECTURE.md §5.1`):** Level 0 splits into `crates/calc-formula-kernel` (pure, stateless parsing, completion, read-only `Present` lens, `Interpret`, `interpret_entry`, and localized formatting) and `crates/calc-formula-host` (stateful single-expression trials, step-by-step evaluation traces, formula-bearing conditional formats, `.xll`/COM/`.xlam`/VBA UDF providers, RTD topic admission, and Explorer session files—moving ~5,940 lines out of `apps/dna-onecalc` and retiring OneCalc's separate 49-message `port.rs` and duplicate shell) `[FRAMING-0040, FRAMING-0043, D-81]`. Enforced by `tools/xtask/architecture.json` and `cargo xtask sweep`, `calc-formula-host`, `dna-view-explorer`, `dna-formula-ui`, `dna-onecalc`, and `dna-onecalc-desktop` **never reach `calc-engine` or `calc-host`**, while Level 1 (`calc-host`) embeds `FormulaHost` without copying it `[FRAMING-0025, FRAMING-0040, FRAMING-0042, FRAMING-0043, D-81]`.
2. **Ships early in Milestone 1 as a public teaser (`D-02`):** Standalone **DNA Calc - Formula Explorer** (`dot-long` cyan-and-petrol Strand identity mark `[•━]`, distinguishing a workbookless scratchpad from file-backed `twin` workbooks) ships in Milestone 1 right after the `calc-skin` foundation so analysts and add-in authors can test DNA Calc's Excel-exact function library, number/conditional formatting, and `.xll` workbench immediately `[FRAMING-0051, FRAMING-0167, P19-CARBON-0020, D-02]`.
3. **One identical workbench in three places (`CL-09-02`, `R-35`):** Carrying forward legacy editor caret and bounded large-array preview rules `[FRAMING-0075, FRAMING-0076]`, one `FormulaView` workbench (`Steps / Live Tree`, `Functions`, `Number Format`, `Conditional Format`, `Add-ins`) mounts identically in:
   - **Standalone desktop & browser builds** (`dna-onecalc-desktop`, `onecalc.dnacalc.com`, `<=0.5 s` cold start, zero workbook loaded, `C-9`) `[P19-CARBON-0065, P19-CARBON-0215, P19-NEON-0057, P19-NEON-0225]`;
   - **Docked inside DNA Calc** (`Alt+Shift+E`, `Shift+F3`, or `Alt,M,V` on any cell, seeded via `TrialFor { at }`) `[P19-CARBON-0065, P19-NEON-0057, P19-NEON-0225]`;
   - **Embedded live boxes on `dnacalc.com` and `handbook.dnacalc.com`** (`<div id="onecalc" data-link="#f=...">`, §1.2.3).

Every AST node in `Steps` (`C-7`–`C-10`, `R-69`) highlights its exact formula character span on click/arrow and reports its intermediate `CalcValue`, Excel type/coercion, spill dimensions, and evaluation microseconds, pinpointing the first sub-expression that produced an `#Error` `[P19-CARBON-0065, P19-CARBON-0213, P19-CARBON-0214, P19-FABLE-0481, P19-NEON-0247, P19-NEON-0248]`.

## 9.2 Standalone Workbench: Caller-Context Honesty and `Local Test Inputs` (`CL-09-03`, `D-70`)

See [Pro 21 F07 — Standalone Formula Explorer](http://127.0.0.1:8720/p21/formula-explorer.html#F07) and [Pro 21 S13 — Caller-dependent refusal](http://127.0.0.1:8720/p21/formula-explorer.html#S13).

1. **Zero fabricated workbook or caller (`D-70`, `R-39`, `Push-back #5`):** Standalone Explorer has **no** worksheet, sheet tabs, or hidden `A1` caller `[P19-GPT-0024, P19-GPT-0269, P21-0082]`. Calling a caller- or workbook-dependent function (`ROW()`, `CELL("address")`, `DNA.CALLERADDRESS()`, `DNA.CallerRow()`) returns an explicit Level-0 `Refusal` host state—**never** disguised as `#REF!`/`#VALUE!` and **never** evaluated against a fake `A1` cell—preserving the user's draft with a one-click **`Open in DNA Calc`** handoff (`#f=...`) `[P19-GPT-0024, P19-GPT-0269, P21-0082, D-70]`.
2. **Literal line-1 speed + badged `Local Test Inputs` shelf (`D-70`, `R-27`):** Launching standalone Explorer (`<=0.5 s`, `<50 ms` warm) lets users immediately evaluate literal formulas (`=PMT(6.5%/12,360,-250000)` -> `1,580.17`) and multi-line `LET`/`LAMBDA` expressions (`Shift+Alt+F` auto-indent), **plus** bind named scalars, small 2D arrays, and `#Error` test values in a clearly badged **Local Test Inputs** shelf (stored in `calc-fml`'s Level-0 name dictionary rather than a hidden spreadsheet; full multi-cell chains graduate to DNA Calc's **Notebook**) `[P19-CARBON-0042, P19-CARBON-0067, P19-CARBON-0198, P19-FABLE-0096, P19-NEON-0038, D-70]`.

## 9.3 Docked In-Workbook Explorer: Isolated Trials, Downstream Preview, and Stale Guards (`CL-09-04`, `CL-09-05`, `D-34`, `D-71`)

See [Pro 21 F06 — Docked Formula Explorer](http://127.0.0.1:8720/p21/formula-explorer.html#F06).

- **Precedent snapshot & trial isolation (`R-37`, `R-68`, `C-15`):** Pressing **`Alt+Shift+E`** (`Shift+F3` -> `Functions`, `Alt,M,V` -> `Steps`) on a workbook cell (`Budget!Summary!D14` or `Sales!Report!D9`) passes a Level-1 `TrialFor` snapshot (`formula`, real `CallerSnapshot`, precedent `Shown<CellValue>`s, `Stamp`, number/CF rules) down into `FormulaView` `[P19-CARBON-0066, P19-FABLE-0067, P19-FABLE-0103, P19-FABLE-0149, P19-FABLE-0156, P19-FABLE-0480, P19-GPT-0045, P19-GPT-0267, P19-GPT-0314, P21-0081]`. Inside the multi-line editor, **`Ctrl+Enter` evaluates the trial** (`Enter` inserts newlines cleanly; `F9` evaluates a selected part; `F4` cycles anchors; `F6` cycles Explorer regions), and overriding precedents in the **`Try`** column (`$50k -> $75k`) stays 100% isolated (`Trying · Workbook unchanged`; `Esc` discards) `[P19-CARBON-0066, P19-FABLE-0067, P19-FABLE-0149, P19-FABLE-0156, P19-FABLE-0480, P21-0081, D-34]`.
- **Level-1 `Preview Impact` & conflict-checked `Apply to cell` (`D-71`, `R-38`):** Explorer itself remains strictly Level-0; when the user clicks **`Preview Impact`** or **`Apply to cell` (`Ctrl+Shift+Enter`)**, the outer Level-1 `dna-shell` previews downstream workbook deltas (`PreviewDepth::WithEffects`) and dispatches a `Batch` with `base` and `expects: vec![cell]`—refusing stale overwrites if `Summary!D14` changed meanwhile (`R-38`) and committing as one shared `Ctrl+Z` step `[P19-GPT-0045, P19-GPT-0268, P21-0081, D-71]`.
