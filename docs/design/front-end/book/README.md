## Purpose and Scope

This book specifies **one coherent front-end design** for **DNA Calc** and its standalone formula workbench **DNA Calc - Formula Explorer** (`skin-0`). Where the source designs overlapped or disagreed across more than 100,000 words of briefs, multi-model proposals, and mockups, this specification makes one explicit choice per point and records every rejected, superseded, or deferred idea in [Appendix Z: Ideas Not Taken](appendix-z-ideas-not-taken.md) with its governing decision and reason.

1. **The engine and file stay plain Excel.** DNA Calc does no more than Excel in its calculation engine, matches Excel to the last bit, and writes standard `.xlsx` and `.xlsm` files that open and calculate in Microsoft Excel without repair.
2. **One workbook, five views, two tools, one shell.** A workbook is read and edited through **Classic** (`Ctrl+Alt+1`), **Inspector** (`Ctrl+Alt+2`), **Notebook** (`Ctrl+Alt+3`), **Model map** (`Ctrl+Alt+4`), and **App** (`Alt+Q` / `Alt+Shift+5`), accompanied by two tools: **Formula Explorer** (standalone `skin-0` on host Level 0, and docked inside DNA Calc via `Alt+Shift+E` / `Shift+F3`) and **Forms maker** (authoring `dna.page/1` pages over the live `PageRenderer`). Every surface sits inside one shared Strand v0.2 shell (`dna-shell`) with one active selection and one undo history.
3. **One central skin contract (`crates/calc-skin`).** Views are small compiled Rust compositions over neutral budgeted queries, atomic frames, and Excel-equivalent model changes; skins (`dna.skin/1`) and pages (`dna.page/1`) are pure JSON declarations with zero executable code in workbook files; custom UI widgets run as signed Rust-to-Wasm Worker extensions (`.dnaext`) over bound slots.
4. **Website hook and bidirectional product story.** Replacing `"A spreadsheet for your Excel workbooks."`, DNA Calc leads with **`"Excel to the last bit. Five ways to see your model."`**—making the UI the immediate first impression while foregrounding headless CLI and Rust engine use, VBA and `.xll`/`.xlam` automation, Windows/browser/Linux reach, and safe agentic customisation, with two-way `#fragment` deep links, embedded live `skin-0` formulas, and shared cast workbooks linking the app and `dnacalc.com`.

## Shared Cast Across Book, App, Website, and Tests

Every chapter, website walkthrough, `"New from example"` app starter, and automated browser smoke test shares five canonical workbooks and six personas (`D-91`):

- **Workbooks:** `Budget.xlsx` (12 assumptions, Base/Stretch scenarios, 12 monthly columns, `216,000`/yr operating profit), `Sales.xlsx` (25,000-row `Orders` table, `SO10482` `4,200` Pending discrepancy on `Report!B5`, `LARK` RTD price, PivotTable), `Loan.xlsx` (`1,000,000` at `10.5%` over `360` months, `9,147.39` payment, Stop validation, balance chart), `Stock.xlsx` (inventory systems model separating 3-day `INDEX` delivery lag `H15->C18` from iterative reserve `B8`, plus `Stock (ticked)` driven by `DnaCalc.Clock`), and `Inherited.xlsm` (Enron-class 40-sheet / 6-hidden / 300,000-formula stress workbook with broken `Revenue.Window` `#REF!`, missing `Rates2024.xlsx`, `OFFSET` names, and quarantined `LegacyClose` macro).
- **Personas:** **Maria** (finance analyst and Excel power user), **Sam** (auditor/reviewer), **Lee** (systems engineer), **Jo** (manager operating models built by others), **Ade** (Excel `.xll`/`.xlam` add-in author), and **an AI agent** collaborating with Maria over `calc-skin`.
