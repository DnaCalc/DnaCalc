## Z.0 Accounting Principles and Major Categories of Rejection

To keep the DNA Calc Design Book **one coherent specification** rather than an overlapping compromise, every Stage-1 idea card in [ideas.jsonl](../ledger/ideas.jsonl) was either adopted into a single canonical mechanism or placed here with its governing decision (`D-01`–`D-93` or [SKIN_ARCHITECTURE.md](../SKIN_ARCHITECTURE.md)) and explicit rationale:

1. **Owner & Decider Rulings (`DECISIONS_20261126.md`, `D-01`–`D-16`):** Rejects custom calculation-engine simulation ticks/directors (`D-03`), promising general VBA macro `Ctrl+Z` in release 1 (`D-04`), Author/Read mode splits in Notebook (`D-06`), immediate FerroUI native rewrite (`D-07`), micro-step migration of the 6 prototype skins (`D-10`), simplified non-Excel key-tip letters (`D-11`), `Ctrl+1..5` or `Ctrl+Alt+5` view-chord collisions (`D-12`), and hidden `_dna.*` defined-name anchors (`D-13`).
2. **Epistemic & Calculation Honesty (Push-backs `#1`–`#5`):** Rejects animated value/wave pulses without recorded host `CalcEvents` (`D-19`), fake numeric confidence percentages on pattern detection (`D-75`), claiming UI display pause stops an upstream RTD server (`D-78`), auto-converting shaded ranges into Excel Tables (`D-77`), and claiming arbitrary dynamic VBA can be statically preflighted or atomically rolled back (`D-88`).
3. **Security, Privacy, and Sandbox Integrity (`SKIN_ARCHITECTURE.md`):** Rejects executable scripts/HTML/YAML in `.xlsx` skins (`D-83`, `D-84`), DOM Web Components in place of signed Wasm-in-Worker `.dnaext` extensions (`SKIN-ARCH §9`), silently rebinding a user's keyboard or contrast tokens on workbook open (`D-82`), and embeddingLevel-0 inside `calc-host` behind a Cargo feature flag (`D-81`).
4. **Scale & Numeric Harmonisation (`D-79`, `D-80`, `D-91`, `D-93`):** Consolidates competing per-model graph caps onto `200 nodes / 400 edges`, unifies WebView2/Leptos latency targets vs ceilings, pins all storyboards to GPT/Pro 21's verified cast arithmetic (`D-91`), and aligns milestone waves onto `M0`–`M8` (`D-93`).
