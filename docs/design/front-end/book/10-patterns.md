## 10.1 Pattern Philosophy: Qualitative Provenance, Four Lifecycle States, and Zero Fake Percentages (`CL-10-01`, `CL-10-02`, `D-74`, `D-75`)

See [Pro 21 W01 — Inherited workbook structure](http://127.0.0.1:8720/p21/workbook.html#W01) and [Pro 21 W02 — Budget Monthly · Why suggested & confirm](http://127.0.0.1:8720/p21/workbook.html#W02).

DNA Calc reads what the workbook file already contains first, asks second, and always allows one-gesture confirmation or correction—so non-grid views work out of the box on plain `.xlsx` files without upfront schemas `[FRAMING-0125, P19-CARBON-0069, P19-FABLE-0031, P19-NEON-0022]`. Non-blocking structural analysis (`Structure` query over OOXML objects, R1C1 formula equivalence classes, precedent/dependent topology, serial-date/number-format axes, and Defined Names/Data Validation—never brittle label regexes) produces **Workbook Readings** governed by four strict rules (`D-74`, `D-75`) `[P19-NEON-0059]`:

1. **Four lifecycle states & three qualitative evidence grades (`D-74`, `R-75`):** Every reading carries one of **four explicit states**—**`From workbook`**, **`Suggested`**, **`Confirmed by <user>`**, or **`Needs review after change`**—paired with one of **three qualitative evidence grades** mapped directly to Strand line style (`Certain / E` = explicit OOXML object/setting -> **solid**; `Structural / S` = R1C1 run, equal-step serial dates, lookup shape -> **dashed**; `Weak / W` = semantic role guess -> **dotted**; weak messes stay unclassified raw ranges) `[FRAMING-0154, P19-FABLE-0161, P19-FABLE-0162, P19-FABLE-0225, P19-FABLE-0487, P19-GPT-0047, P19-GPT-0334, P19-NEON-0110, P21-0088, D-74]`. **Invented numeric confidence percentages (`"92% confidence"`) are forbidden** `[P19-GPT-0047, P21-0088, D-74]`.
2. **Composition & Classic non-interference:** One region may hold multiple readings simultaneously (e.g., `Loan!A12:F371` is a time series + running balance + R1C1 formula run), the highest-certainty reading governs default display, interior exceptions are **always surfaced** rather than absorbed, and **Classic never alters cell rendering because of a pattern reading** (surfacing readings only in `Details`, `Outline`, and `<40%` Semantic Zoom) `[P19-FABLE-0164, P19-FABLE-0575]`.
3. **CustomXML-only confirmation vs. explicit Workbook Edits (`D-75`, `W02`):** Clicking **`Why suggested`** (`W02` on `Budget!Monthly!B1:M11`) shows concrete evidence (`12 real serial dates in row 1, 48 repeated formulas in B8:M11, 0 overrides`) while noting repetition alone does not certify business meaning `[P19-GPT-0054, P21-0090]`. Confirming, splitting, merging, adjusting header rows, reclassifying (`Ctrl+Shift+B`), or dismissing writes **exclusively to `<readings>` in `urn:dnacalc:views:v1`** (`0` cells, formulas, names, Tables, or visibility flags changed); subsequent edits to headers/formulas flip the reading to `Needs review after change` (and external edits reconcile as `Relocated` or `Orphan`, `R-77`); while real workbook mutations (`Define Name…`, `Convert to Table Ctrl+T`) sit strictly below a **`Workbook edits`** divider `[P19-CARBON-0071, P19-CARBON-0115, P19-FABLE-0489, P19-GPT-0054, P19-NEON-0061, P21-0088, P21-0090, D-75]`.
4. **Bounded `Workbook Structure` overview & explicit sampling (`W01`, `CL-10-03`):** `W01` (`Inherited`) summarises sheet counts, formula counts, hidden sheets (never revealing hidden cell contents), and recognised patterns even while dependency resolution is partial (`18/40 sheets`), backed by budgeted `Structure` and `Census` queries (`C-6`, `C-14`, `C-16`, `C-47`–`C-50`), while explicit row `Sample` tools (`200 of 25,000 rows`) never feed exact totals (`Enron`: `15,770` analyzable files, `20.28M` formulas) `[P19-CARBON-0212, P19-FABLE-0571, P19-FABLE-0572, P19-FABLE-0573, P19-FABLE-0574, P19-GPT-0055, P19-NEON-0252, P19-NEON-0254, P21-0089]`.
