DNA Calc in the browser

Open an Excel workbook from your device and recalculate its formulas in a browser tab.

Open local previewDownload the sample workbook

Open the sample and switch to the Calc worksheet. The formula in A1 uses SEQUENCE to fill a range. Calculation runs in WebAssembly in your browser. The page opens files you select and saves through browser downloads.
