Automation

Your macros and add-ins come with the workbook

A real Excel model is formulas plus the VBA and add-ins around them. DNA Calc runs a workbook's VBA project with its own VBA runtime, CalcVBA, and loads native XLL add-ins on Windows.

Three macro levels where Excel has two

Excel asks one question about a macro workbook: enable or not. DNA Calc has a level between.

LevelWhat happens
OffThe default. The workbook opens with its project held. You enable it per file.
GuardedThe project runs, and calls to dangerous operating-system services are refused.
UnguardedThe project runs with full access, as Excel's enabled macros do.

From a script the level is pinned for one run:

dnacalc run book.xlsm --macro Module1.MyFunction --arg 4 --macro-level guarded

What is there and not yet measured here

  • XLL and COM add-ins need Windows x64. In the browser and on Linux they are reported as not available on that host. See Platforms.
  • Third-party VBA projects are recorded with their mismatches, refusals and missing Excel answers in the VBA corpus table.
