### Seasonal covariance: direct innovation gamma survives a bit-level discrimination

The frozen covariance jumps distinguish `(a+p*b+g)^2` from the traditional-gamma expression

The fresh follow-up makes the test exact. For each of C1..C9 and B5, enumerate binary64 v
whose rounded sqrt equals the observed endpoint RMS and whose final stored-PC64 95% product
equals C1. Freeze all resulting outputs at h=p+2,2p+1,3p+1, for both confidence levels.
For **each history, one common v (sometimes two) explains all six outputs bit for bit** under
direct gamma. No traditional-gamma prediction explains any of those 60 outputs. The surviving
v bit sets are in `results-summary.json`; they are rounding preimages, not fitted constants.

**Implementable normalized covariance computation** for a later admitted noisy seasonal fit,
at positive integer h and effective period p, with reported gains a,b,g:

```text
k = floor((h - 1) / p)
V = 1 + (h - 1) * ((h*a*b + h*(2*h - 1)*b*b/6) + a*a)
V = V + g*k * ((2*a + g) + p*b*(k + 1))
q = (1 + confidence) / 2
z = 1 if q == 1 else the existing inverse-normal kernel(q)
CI = stored_PC64_multiply(z, sqrt(v * V))
```

Retain the displayed binary64 operation grouping; do not scale gamma by 1-alpha. At endpoint,
q==1 supplies z=1. The frozen 95% multiplier is `1.9599639845400536`. For the tested positive
n8/p2 impulse family, v is the ordinary application SSE/n exactly; its general seasonal divisor
is not identified by normalized covariance alone. **Rows:** this is the interval formula needed
by the nine refused noisy seasonal confidence families once their fit is known, and the 60 new
confidence outputs here. It changes no currently calculated zero-innovation seasonal result
and cannot itself unlock a refused model. Do not generalize a reset/variance divisor from it.
