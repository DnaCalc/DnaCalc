Spill Range

Drop the arrays

Each piece is a dynamic-array formula spilling numbers down the sheet. Fill a row across A:J and it is summed, scored and cleared. When a new spill has nowhere to go, you get #SPILL!.

The sheet counts up: each new spill takes the next numbers, and its formula says which (=SEQUENCE(2,2,9) holds 9 to 12). A cleared row scores its SUM times the level; clear several at once for a bonus.
