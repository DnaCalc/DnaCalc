Grow a formula

Your formula is a chain of cells. Steer it into numbers to add them to the SUM. Run into yourself and you have made a circular reference. Error cells are poison.

Gold cells hold =PI(). Some cells hold 0.1, and 0.1 + 0.2 is not quite 0.3 in binary64: the formula bar shows what Excel shows, the bits show the truth.
